# Code of Conduct

MailKeys follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be patient and assume good intent. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@kent](https://github.com/kent), privately. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads or block contributors who break this code.
